`Webpage.docx` is a single content table with these labeled rows: **Organization Name, Logo, Registration No., Tagline, About Us** (one long cell containing the org description, "Our Journey & Genesis," "Our Sanctuary," "Beyond the Shelter: Our Pillars of Service," "Sustainability & Transparency," and "How You Can Help: Next Steps" — all as one continuous narrative), **HISTORY** (label only, body empty), **VISION** (three pillar statements), **MISSION** (three pillar statements), **Founder Details** (5 names/titles), **Managing Committee Details** (a nested 27-row table: Name, Designation, Mobile Number), **TREASURERS MESSAGE** (label only, body empty), **ADDRESS** (label only, body empty), **Google Maps Location** (label only, body empty), **Working Hours** (label only, body empty). An embedded logo image (`word/media/image1.png`) accompanies the "Logo" row.

The document does **not** contain: Gallery, News, Events, Testimonials, Bank Details, PAN, distinct Donation Category pricing tables, or Contact phone/email content — these checklist items were verified against what already exists on the site (built in earlier phases from other verified sources), not against this document, since it says nothing about them.

Per the explicit instruction governing this audit, **no content is fabricated**: where the document has a labeled section with no body text (History, Treasurer's Message, Address, Google Map, Working Hours), the corresponding site section is left exactly as it was — there is nothing to synchronize.
