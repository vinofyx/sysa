Business Requirements Document (BRD)Sai Yadadri Seva Ashram – Website & Donation Platform
Prepared for: Sai Yadadri Seva AshramPrepared by: Vinofyx
1. Project Objective
Develop a modern, responsive website with an integrated donation/e-commerce system, admin dashboard, volunteer management, reports, bilingual support (English/Telugu), SEO, and secure payment gateway.
2. Website Modules
Home (Hero banner, welcome message, quick donate, events, testimonials)
About Us (History, Vision, Mission, Founder, Committee, Treasurer Message)
Activities (Vanaprastha Ashram, Wellness Centre, Annaprasadam, Goshala, Daily Sevas, Medical Camps)
Donations (UPI, Razorpay, Bank Transfer)
Donation Categories (Annaprasadam, Gosala, Old Age Home, Building Fund, General Donation)
Donor Corner (Major donors, Monthly donors, CSR partners)
Events & News
Gallery (Photo & Video)
Volunteer Registration & Internship
Reports & Transparency
Appeals & Current Needs
Contact Us & Social Media
Admin Dashboard
3. Functional Requirements
